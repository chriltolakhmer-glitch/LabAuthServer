# Contributing

> DRAFT — REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL LEGAL REVIEW

LabAuthServer is currently vendor-controlled proprietary software. No external source-code contributions are accepted initially.

## Current Policy

- Unsolicited source-code pull requests are not currently accepted.
- The repository remains private and controlled.
- Bug reports may be accepted through an approved vendor channel.
- Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- No contributor-license agreement or contributor IP framework is created by this policy.
- The contribution and intellectual-property policy may be reconsidered in a later approved phase.

This document does not grant contributor rights or create a license to use, modify, redistribute, or commercially deploy the source.
